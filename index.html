VIDHI SALES

VIDHI SALES, established in 2009, is a leading supplier of housekeeping equipment, cleaning chemicals, and kitchen and washroom hygiene products in Goa. We are the authorized distributor in Goa for leading international brands such as FILMOP (Italy), UNGER (Germany) and REINLICH (France).

We provide durable, ergonomically designed and cost-effective equipment and mechanised deep cleaning solutions for floors, glass, kitchens and washrooms, along with a colour-coding system to prevent cross contamination. Our customers include pharmaceutical units, resorts, spas, hospitals, showrooms and restaurants.

Our products carry the required international certificates for audits. We also provide housekeeping staff training and after-sales service.
